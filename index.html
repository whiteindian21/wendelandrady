Why It Works

Why Static
Infrastructure Wins

Page builders are designed to be easy to edit. They were never designed to be fast. Every visual editor, plugin, and drag-and-drop block adds a layer your visitor has to download and render before they ever see your offer. Static infrastructure removes that layer entirely.

  • No Builder Runtime
    Static HTML ships exactly what the browser needs — nothing assembled client-side.
  • Vercel's Edge Network
    Your page is served from the location closest to each visitor, every time.
  • Supabase for Lead Data
    Your leads land in a database you control — not locked inside a builder's export tool.
  • Near-Zero Hosting Cost
    No builder subscription, no plugin licenses — hosting alone costs next to nothing.
